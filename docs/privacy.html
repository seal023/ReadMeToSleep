Read Me To Sleep · Bedtime Stories for Kids

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

Read Me To Sleep ("the App") is a bedtime story application for children aged 3–8, designed to let parents read stories using their own voices. We take the privacy of children and families very seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

In short: Story and voice features require sending the minimal necessary content (your text inputs, voice recordings you actively make) to AI services. Accounts are only used to restore membership across devices. We do not serve ads, do not build user profiles, and do not sell any data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Login Info (When Using Login Features)

You can use the built-in offline classic story library without signing in. Signing in is required for AI story generation, voice cloning, and travel check-in, because these features involve server-side daily quotas and account-bound entitlements. If you choose to sign in:

  • Sign in with Apple: We receive an anonymous user identifier from Apple (to recognize your account), and the name/email you choose to share. If you select "Hide My Email", we only receive a relay address (@privaterelay.appleid.com).
  • Email OTP Login: We collect your email address to send a 6-digit verification code.
  • Session Tokens: Login credentials are stored on your device and maintained on our servers so you don't have to log in every time.

1.2 Child's Nickname and Preferences

We ask for your child's nickname to personalize greetings and story content. This is stored locally and, if logged in, synced to your account for recovery across devices. Please avoid entering the child's full real name, school, or address.

1.3 Story Content Inputs

Themes, character names, details, and optional sensitive word settings are sent to our AI service (Alibaba Cloud Bailian) for story generation.

1.4 Voice and Audio Data

Whether audio is uploaded depends on the feature. Please see the table below:

FeatureLeaves Device?Purpose
Voice Cloning (recording parents' voice) Yes (to Alibaba Cloud) To create your exclusive voice model for reading stories
Voice Input / Voice Q&A / Story Speech-to-Text Yes (to Alibaba Cloud) For speech recognition. We only receive the text result
Playback of saved story recordings No Saved locally on your device for playback
Important: When using "Voice Q&A", "Voice Input", or "Speech-to-Text", the audio recorded (which may be the child's voice) will be uploaded to Alibaba Cloud for recognition. We only receive the text result and do not retain the audio on our servers; however, please be aware this upload occurs.

1.5 Location Info (Optional)

Collected only when you actively use "Location-based Story Generation" or "Travel Check-in" to associate stories with locations. It is never used for ads, cross-app tracking, or sharing movement history.

1.6 What We DO NOT Collect

  • Contacts, SMS, photo library, calendar, or unrelated data.
  • Advertising Identifiers (IDFA) for cross-app tracking.
  • Payment details (processed entirely by Apple App Store).
  • Precise continuous location tracks.

2. Voice Cloning: How Your Voice is Processed

"Read with Mom/Dad's Voice" is our core feature. Here is how it works:

  1. You record a voice sample and tap "Confirm & Submit".
  2. The sample is uploaded to Alibaba Cloud Bailian to generate an exclusive voice model.
  3. When generating story audio, we send the story text and your model ID to retrieve the audio.
  4. This model is only used for you within the App. We do not use it for other users, ads, or secondary distribution.
Important Notice:
  • Only record your own voice or an adult who has given explicit consent.
  • Voice is sensitive personal information. Do not let untrusted individuals use your device.
  • To delete uploaded voice samples and generated models, contact us via the email at the end of this policy. We will process requests within 30 days.

3. How We Use Information

  • To generate bedtime story texts and audio.
  • To personalize greetings using the child's nickname.
  • To maintain your login and restore membership benefits.
  • To troubleshoot and improve quality (using anonymized aggregate data only).

We do not use this information for ads, profiling, or marketing, nor do we sell or rent it to third parties.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • On Your Device: Stories, nicknames, recordings, settings, cached audio. Uninstalling deletes all.
  • On Account Servers: Account ID, email, nickname, session. Used strictly for login and cross-device recovery.
  • Sent to AI Services: Story inputs, voice data. Processed minimally; no long-term content profiles are built.
  • Membership Data: Subscriptions are managed by Apple. We only store plan and expiration dates locally to display benefits.

5. Third-Party Services

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for children, but accounts must be created and managed by parents or guardians. Children do not hold accounts directly. We comply with COPPA, GDPR for children, and related laws.
  • Registration requires adult action (email or Apple ID).
  • No behavioral ads or third-party ads are shown. The App is ad-free.
  • We do not collect children's personally identifiable information for cross-app tracking.
  • Nicknames and recordings are only used for stated features and parents can delete them anytime.
  • If we discover unintended collection of children's data without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

  • Access & Correct: Via "Parent Center → Account & Credentials".
  • Delete Local Data: Delete individual stories/recordings or uninstall the App.
  • Delete Account: Via "Parent Center → Account & Credentials → Delete Account" to permanently delete your account and associated data.
  • Delete Voice Data: Email us to delete uploaded samples and models.
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop using features or log out. The App works without logging in.

8. Data Security

Data is transmitted via HTTPS. Server access is protected by row-level security, meaning accounts can only access their own data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

9. Policy Updates

For material changes, we will update the version and date here and notify you in-app.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact seal023@163.com. We will reply within 30 days.

隐私政策

Read Me To Sleep · 儿童睡前故事

Read Me To Sleep（以下简称「本 App」）是一款为 3–8 岁儿童生成睡前故事的应用，核心功能是让家长用自己的声音为孩子朗读故事。我们非常重视儿童与家庭的隐私保护，本政策说明我们如何收集、使用、存储与保护您的信息。

一句话概括：故事与语音功能需要把我们收到的最小必要内容（您输入的文字、您主动录制的语音）发送给 AI 服务处理；账号只用于让会员跨设备找回。我们不做广告投放、不做用户画像、不出售任何数据。

一、我们收集的信息

1. 账号与登录信息（使用登录功能时）

不登录也可以使用内置的离线经典故事库等基础功能。AI 故事生成、声音克隆、旅行打卡需要登录后使用，因为这些功能涉及服务端每日额度与绑定账号的权益。当您选择登录时：

  • 使用「通过 Apple 登录」：我们会收到 Apple 提供的一个匿名用户标识符（用于识别同一账号），以及您选择共享的姓名与邮箱。若您选择 Apple 的「隐藏我的邮箱」，我们只能收到一个 Apple 生成的中转地址（@privaterelay.appleid.com），无法得知您的真实邮箱。
  • 使用邮箱验证码登录：我们会收集您填写的邮箱地址，用于向您发送 6 位登录验证码。
  • 登录会话令牌：为让您不必每次打开都重新登录，登录凭证会保存在您的设备上，并在我们的账号服务端维持会话。

2. 孩子的昵称与偏好

首次使用时会请您填写「希望我们如何称呼您的宝贝」（如小名、昵称）。该昵称用于在问候语、故事与界面中称呼孩子，并用于个性化故事内容。

该昵称保存在您的设备上；若您已登录，也会同步保存到您的账号下，以便换设备时恢复。请避免填写孩子的真实全名、学校、住址等可识别身份的信息。

3. 您输入的故事内容

您输入的故事主题、主角名、细节要求、旅行打卡地点，以及可选的「敏感词」设置。这些内容会发送到我们的 AI 服务（阿里云百炼）用于生成故事文本。

4. 录音与语音数据

录音是否上传，取决于您使用哪个功能。请您留意下表：

功能录音是否离开设备用途
声音克隆（录制家长/亲人的声音样本） 会上传至阿里云百炼 生成属于您的专属声音模型，让故事用您的声音朗读
语音输入 / 语音提问 / 讲故事录音转文字 会上传至阿里云百炼做语音识别 把语音转成文字，用于搜索、提问或保存成文字故事。我们只取回文字结果
普通录音回放（已保存的故事音频） 不上传 仅保存在您的设备上，供您随时回放
请特别注意：当您使用「语音提问」「语音输入」或「讲故事录音转文字」时，麦克风录到的声音（可能是孩子的声音）会被上传到阿里云百炼进行语音识别。我们只接收识别出的文字结果，不会在服务器上另行留存这段音频；但请知晓这一上传行为确实发生。

5. 位置信息（可选）

仅当您主动使用「基于位置生成故事」或旅行打卡功能时获取，用于把故事与所在地点关联，不会用于广告或跨应用追踪，也不会上传您的移动轨迹。

6. 我们不会收集的信息

  • 通讯录、短信、相册、日历等与功能无关的数据；
  • 广告标识符（IDFA），我们不做跨应用追踪；
  • 支付卡号、银行账户等支付信息（支付全部由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不接触）；
  • 精确的持续位置轨迹。

二、声音克隆：您的语音会怎样被处理

「用爸爸妈妈的声音讲」是本 App 的核心功能，其工作原理如下，请您在使用前了解：

  1. 您主动在 App 内录制一段声音样本并点击「确认并提交克隆」；
  2. 该样本会上传至阿里云百炼（Alibaba Cloud Bailian）的声音复刻服务，生成一个与该声音特征绑定的专属声音模型；
  3. 此后生成故事语音时，我们向同一服务发送故事文本与您的声音模型标识，取回朗读音频；
  4. 该声音模型仅用于为您本人朗读本 App 内的故事，我们不会将其用于其他用户、广告、配音售卖或任何二次分发。
重要提醒：
  • 请只录制您本人或已获得明确同意的成年人的声音，不要录制他人的声音；
  • 声音属于敏感个人信息，一旦生成声音模型即可合成该声音说话。请勿将设备交给不信任的人使用；
  • 您可以随时停止使用该功能。如需删除已上传的声音样本与已生成的声音模型，请通过本政策末尾的联系邮箱告知我们，我们会在收到请求后 30 日内处理并回复。

  • 把您的输入转化为睡前故事文本与朗读语音；
  • 识别并称呼孩子的昵称，提供更贴合的陪伴体验；
  • 维持您的登录状态、绑定并恢复您的会员权益；
  • 排查故障、改进故事与语音质量（仅使用去标识化的汇总信息）。

我们不会将上述任何信息用于广告投放、用户画像、信用评估或商业营销，也不会出售、出租给第三方。

  • 会员与交易信息：订阅由 Apple 管理；我们在设备上保存订阅方案与到期时间，用于展示权益与凭证，不保存任何支付凭证号码。
